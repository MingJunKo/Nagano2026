Before you fly

Do these in Singapore. Ticks are saved on this phone.

1Plane to arrivals hall

60–80 min · don't commit to a train until you're through

  1. Note your terminal (T1, T2 or T3). Station names: "Narita Airport Terminal 1" or "Airport Terminal 2·3".
  2. Follow yellow Arrivals / 到着 signs, then Immigration.
  3. Immigration: foreign passport lane, Visit Japan Web QR ready, fingerprints + photo.
  4. Baggage claim: screens list carousels by flight number.
  5. Customs: scan the customs QR at the e-gate.
  6. Arrivals lobby (1F): toilets, 7-Eleven ATM, konbini. Grab water and a snack.

2Skyliner to Keisei Ueno

About 1 hour including tickets

Schematic. Layout differs between terminals; follow the blue Keisei signs.

  1. Buy at the blue Skyliner machines or the Skyliner & Keisei Information Center. Cards accepted. Ask: "Skyliner to Ueno, three people, next train, seats together."
  2. Online voucher? Scan its QR at the Skyliner machine and pick a train.
  3. Insert your ticket at the gate and take it back. You need it to exit at Ueno.
  4. Board your car number. Big bags go in racks at the end of each car.
Wrong-train trap: cheaper Keisei trains (Access Express, Limited Express) leave from the same area and are much slower. Board only a train marked Skyliner.
Don't get off at Nippori. Stay on to Keisei Ueno, the last stop.

3Book the Shinkansen on the Skyliner

Use the 40-min ride to lock in 3 seats together

  1. Open JR-EAST Train Reservation and log in.
  2. From Ueno 上野 to Karuizawa 軽井沢, Sat 7 Nov, 3 adults. Leave at least 20 min after your Skyliner arrives.
  3. Pick あさま Asama or はくたか Hakutaka. Never かがやき Kagayaki.
  4. Ordinary car, reserved seat 指定席, three seats side by side.
  5. Suitcase over 160 cm? Reserve a "seat with oversized baggage area" for that person.
  6. Choose paper tickets. You collect them at a green JR East machine at JR Ueno.
  7. Paper-ticket booking closes 6 min before departure. Screenshot the confirmation QR.
No signal, or card rejected? Just buy at the machines by the JR Ueno Central Gate. A rejected card doesn't mean the train is full.

4Keisei Ueno → Shinkansen platform

20–25 min with bags · two separate stations, then four floors down

Part A · Street walk (3–5 min)

Schematic, not to scale. Signs in English are everywhere on this walk.

  1. Insert your Skyliner ticket at the Keisei gate (it keeps it).
  2. Take the Main Exit, left of the gates.
  3. Walk north to JR Ueno, entering by the Shinobazu Exit side.
  4. Head to the Central Gate 中央改札. Avoid the Park and Iriya Exits (upstairs, longer).

Part B · Tickets at the green machines

  1. At the green JR East reserved-seat machines 指定席券売機 by the Central Gate, tap English.
  2. Booked? "Read QR code" or "Pick up tickets", then scan your screenshot.
  3. Not booked? Buy: Ueno → Karuizawa, next Asama/Hakutaka, reserved, 3 adults.
  4. Stuck? The staffed ticket office is right beside the Central Gate.

Part C · Down to the platform (~10 min)

  1. Feed both tickets into the Central Gate, take both back.
  2. The Shinkansen Transfer Gate is on your right just inside. Tickets in again, take them back.
  3. Ride the long escalators down. B3F has ekiben, drinks and benches.
  4. Continue down to B4F, platform 19 or 20 as the board shows.

5Board the right train

The train starts at Tokyo and stops at Ueno only briefly — be lined up first

Example board and car only — your times, car and seat are on your ticket.

  1. Check the board: あさま Asama or はくたか Hakutaka. If it says かがやき Kagayaki, don't board.
  2. Your ticket reads like "6号車 12A" = car 6, row 12, seat A.
  3. Queue at your car's floor marking. Board promptly when doors open.
  4. Small bags overhead; suitcases at the car end or behind the last row.
  5. English announcements name each stop. Karuizawa comes after Omiya and Takasaki, about 60–70 min.
  6. Keep all tickets — the Karuizawa exit gate needs them.

6Arriving at Karuizawa

One set of gates, then North or South Exit

Drop bags in a locker and grab lunch if you like — Tsuruya check-in is 15:00.

If things go wrong

Both trains run often — the worst case is usually a 30–60 min delay

ProblemDo this
Immigration 90+ minTake a later Skyliner (every 20–40 min). Book the Shinkansen only once you're on it.
Skyliner full for an hourTake the Narita Express (JR, red) to Tokyo Station; board the Shinkansen there, platforms 20–23.
No 3 seats togetherTake the next Asama/Hakutaka, or book the Green Car.
Missed your booked trainGo to the ticket office. Reserved tickets usually work in unreserved cars of a later train the same day — confirm there.
Lost in UenoFollow green "Shinkansen" signs, or ask: "Shinkansen wa doko desu ka?"
